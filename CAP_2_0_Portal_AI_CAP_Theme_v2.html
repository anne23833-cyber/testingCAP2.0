🎯 CCK New System Competency Assessment
Competent within 15 days. Each task is assessed across up to five attempts. A staff member must achieve 3 Competent (C) ratings in the same task to be confirmed Competent in that task.
Assessment Rule3 × Competent (C) = Confirmed Competent in that task.
Rating GuideC — correct and confident without supervision
NI — able to perform but still requires guidance
NC — unable to perform correctly
TimelineTarget: confirmed Competent within 15 days from the first assessment attempt.
